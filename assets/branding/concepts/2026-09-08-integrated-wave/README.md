## Original direction prompt

Use case: logo-brand / refined app-icon redesign.
Create ONE beautifully resolved, contemporary AhoiBrowser app icon, a full-bleed square 1:1 bitmap, suitable as the master artwork for iPhone and Mac. One icon only, not a board, mockup or comparison sheet.

REFERENCES: Image 1 is the current MOBILE icon to redesign. Its thick sine-wave line, literal toy sailboat, and translucent background disc are the specific weaknesses: do not retain those shapes. Image 2 is the established DESKTOP brand reference: saturated deep cobalt, warm ivory, a small restrained coral accent, and abstract sails suggesting an A. Preserve this brand character while improving the mobile design. Do NOT simply return either reference unchanged.

ART DIRECTION: confident, minimalist, timeless visual identity for a high-quality native browser. Design a custom sculptural but FLAT ivory sail/current mark whose negative space subtly suggests the letter A, without rendering text. Two elegant, carefully proportioned sail planes and ONE calm sweeping wave/keel form a coherent, compact silhouette. The wave should be a broad, tapering, gently asymmetric current that feels integral to the base of the sails, not a separate thick wavy underline. Use clean, optically balanced curves with smooth transitions and deliberate negative space. A clear slightly rising crest may suggest movement, but NO surf curl, spiral, foam, droplets, multiple ripples, or uniform sine wave. Keep the two sail tips elegant and intact. Add only a small coral circular accent near the upper-left of the mark, as in the desktop brand, with balanced spacing.

COLOR: uniform deep cobalt blue background close to #143DB7. Main mark warm ivory close to #FFF9EA, one small coral accent close to #FF6655. Strictly flat solid colors. No gradient, no glossy bevel, no glass, no glow, no shadow, no noisy texture, no enclosing circular disc or badge. Strong silhouette that will read at small app-icon sizes. High-end vector-like edge precision in a raster image.

COMPOSITION: straight-on, centered and optically balanced; mark occupies roughly 64–68% of canvas width and height, generous even breathing room. Background extends to all four square canvas edges; DO NOT bake rounded outer app-icon corners or a border into the image, the operating system will apply its mask. High resolution, clean edges. No wordmark, no letters, no typography, no watermark, no device frame or scene. The result should feel purpose-designed, not clip art or a stock nautical logo.

## Final refinement prompt

Refine this AhoiBrowser icon. Image 1 is the edit target. Preserve its full square deep cobalt background, warm ivory sails, small coral circular accent, flat minimalist brand language and overall composition. Make ONE specific structural correction to the mark:

The detached white ribbon BELOW the sails must disappear as a separate object. Integrate the wave/keel organically into the lower part of the LARGE RIGHT SAIL instead, so this sail and the one smooth sweeping current are a SINGLE uninterrupted ivory silhouette. The large sail descends into a generous, controlled lower curve sweeping toward the lower left; its end tapers elegantly to a clean point. Keep the smaller left sail as the second ivory plane. Leave enough cobalt negative space under/between the sail planes to preserve the subtle abstract A and the sense of forward motion. At most TWO ivory shapes total, not three. No disconnected wavy underline. No blunt cut, stub or bulb at the wave end. This should feel like a custom-designed abstract sail/current monogram rather than a clip-art sailboat. Keep the wave quiet, fluid and compact, with a single broad sweep, not a repeating sine or surf curl.

Optically center the complete mark with generous safe margins on all sides for an app-icon mask. Refine curve continuity and spacing. Truly FLAT solid colors, no mottling, diagonal texture, shading, gradients, gloss or shadows. Deep cobalt #143DB7, ivory #FFF9EA, coral #FF6655. Background full bleed to all four corners of the square; no baked rounded corners, border, surrounding mockup, words, labels, watermark or extra symbols. Deliver one crisp high-resolution square icon, not a sheet.
